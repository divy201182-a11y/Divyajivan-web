Straight answers

What the app doesn’t do.

We would rather tell you up front.

  • It doesn’t diagnose. It carries the record; your clinician reads it.
  • No payments in the app. Pay at the hospital counter; the receipt appears in the app.
  • Records come from DivyaJivan hospitals. They appear for hospitals and clinics that run on DivyaJivan.
  • Not an emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number or go to a hospital.
